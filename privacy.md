This policy explains what happens to your information when you visit casinocademy.com. We keep it short because we collect very little: there are no accounts, no comments and no newsletter sign-up on this site.

## Who we are

Casinocademy publishes guides about online casino games, bonuses and payment methods. If you have a question about this policy or want to use any of your rights below, email us at [support@casinocademy.com](mailto:support@casinocademy.com).

## What we collect, and why

**Analytics, only if you accept.** When you first visit, a banner asks whether you accept analytics cookies. If you choose Accept, we load Google Tag Manager and Google Analytics 4. They tell us which pages are read and which links are clicked, so we can improve the site. Google Analytics sets cookies in your browser and sends Google information such as your IP address, browser and device type, the pages you view and roughly where you are. If you choose Reject, none of this is loaded and any Google Analytics cookies are deleted.

**Your cookie choice.** We save your Accept or Reject choice in your browser's local storage under the name `cc_consent_v1`, so we do not ask again on every page. It stays on your device and is never sent to us. You can change your mind at any time with the <a href="#" role="button" data-cookie-settings>Cookie settings</a> link here or at the bottom of any page.

**Fonts.** The site's text fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, so your browser connects to Google's servers when a page loads and Google receives your IP address. This happens whatever your cookie choice, because the fonts are needed to display the page.

**Hosting.** The site is hosted by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.). Like any web host, GitHub may log technical information such as your IP address and the pages requested, for security and to run the service. See [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement) for how it handles that data.

**When you email us.** Our contact form does not send anything to a server. It opens your own email app with a message addressed to support@casinocademy.com. If you send it, we receive your email address, your name and what you wrote, and we use them only to reply to you. We keep emails for as long as we need to deal with your question and then delete them.

**Search.** The search box works entirely in your browser. What you type is not sent to us or to anyone else.

## Cookies

| Cookie | Set by | Purpose | Lasts |
|---|---|---|---|
| `_ga`, `_ga_` followed by an ID | Google Analytics | Tells visits and visitors apart so pages can be counted | Up to 2 years |

These are only set if you accept analytics. We do not use advertising cookies, and we do not sell your data.

## Where your data goes

Google and GitHub are US companies, so the information described above may be processed in the United States and other countries. They are responsible for how they handle it under their own policies. See [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## How long we keep it

Analytics data is kept in Google Analytics for a limited period that we set in our account, after which it is deleted automatically. Your cookie choice stays in your browser until you clear your browser data or change it yourself.

## Your choices and rights

You can withdraw your consent to analytics at any time with the Cookie settings link. You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings.

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to ask us what personal data we hold about you, to have it corrected or deleted, to object to how we use it, and to complain to your local data protection authority. Email us at [support@casinocademy.com](mailto:support@casinocademy.com) and we will reply as soon as we can.

## Links to other websites

Our articles link to other sites, such as [Gambling Therapy](https://www.gamblingtherapy.org) and [eCOGRA](https://ecogra.org). They have their own privacy policies. If we add links to casinos or other partners, those sites may set their own cookies and collect their own data when you visit them, and we will update this page to say so.

## Age

This site is for adults. Gambling is for people aged 18 and over (or the legal age where you live), and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone younger.
