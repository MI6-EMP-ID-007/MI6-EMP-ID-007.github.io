<h2>The slot that has made more millionaires than any other</h2>

Round 1 gave us an electric classic. Round 2 is a very different kind of contender. This one does not try to win you over with fancy bonus rounds or a cinematic intro. It wins you over with a number that sits above the reels and keeps on climbing.
<!--more-->
Welcome back to The Ace of Games, our search for the best online Casino game in the world. If you missed the build-up, the links at the bottom of this page will catch you up. Today we are taking a look at the one and only Mega Moolah.

The setup could not be simpler. Mega Moolah is a safari-themed slot with 5 reels, 3 rows and 25 fixed paylines. Lions, elephants, giraffes and zebras fill the reels, and the whole thing looks every bit as old as it is. Microgaming released it back in 2006 and, unlike many games from that era, it never faded away. It never needed to. It has one trick, and it is a very good one.

**Four jackpots, one wheel**

Mega Moolah is a progressive jackpot slot. A small slice of every bet placed on the game, at every casino that offers it, is added to a shared prize pool. That is why the top prize can grow into a sum that no ordinary slot could ever afford to pay.

There are four jackpots in total: Mini, Minor, Major and the mighty Mega. Any spin on the game can trigger the Jackpot Wheel, and once the wheel appears you are guaranteed to win one of the four. Bigger bets give you better odds of landing the bigger jackpots, but you do not need a huge stake to be in with a chance.

**The Lion and the Monkey**

Away from the jackpot, the game keeps things simple. The lion is the wild, and he substitutes for other symbols and doubles the wins he helps to create. The monkey is the scatter. Land three or more of them and you will trigger 15 free spins in which every win is tripled.

It is a solid feature, but let us be honest, it is not the reason anybody plays this game. Compare it with the Great Hall of Spins in [Thunderstruck II]({% post_url 2017-09-06-The-Ace-of-Games %}) and you can see where Microgaming spent its effort.

**Here is the catch**

Remember what we said about win percentages in [RTP and Volatility Explained]({% post_url 2017-09-03-The-Ace-of-Games %})? Mega Moolah is the perfect example of why that number matters. Its RTP is commonly listed at around 88%, which is a lot lower than most slots. Because a part of every bet feeds the jackpot, less of it goes back to you in regular wins.

That does not make it a bad game. It makes it a different kind of game. You are not paying for steady value here, you are paying for a lottery ticket that you can spin. Expect quiet stretches, and treat any session as entertainment first and a shot at the big one second.

**Does it pay?**

Sometimes it pays spectacularly. In 2015 a player at Betway turned a single 25p spin into more than £13 million, a win that Guinness World Records recognised as the largest online slot payout of its time. Those stories are exactly why the game still draws crowds.

**Our verdict for Round 2**

Mega Moolah is not trying to be the best slot at everything, and that makes it hard to score fairly against a game like Thunderstruck II. Judged on regular play and bonus value, it falls short. Judged on the thrill of a life-changing jackpot, nothing else here comes close.
