Every contest needs a contender with some personality. In The Ace of Games that is Immortal Romance, a vampire love story from Microgaming that has been drawing players back since it launched in 2011.

<!--more-->

**The basics**

- **Provider:** Microgaming
- **Layout:** five reels with 243 ways to win
- **Theme:** gothic romance, with four central characters: Amber, Troy, Michael and Sarah

**What makes it special**

Most slots have a free spins round. Immortal Romance gives its round a cast. The Chamber of Spins bonus is tied to the four characters, and as you reach it more often you unlock a different feature for each of them. The base game has its own surprises too, including a random Wild Desire feature that can turn reels wild without warning.

**Why it is in the contest**

Casino lobbies fill up with new games every month, and most are forgotten quickly. A game that is still easy to find years after it launched has usually earned its place, and that staying power is what makes Immortal Romance a serious contender.

**What we will check**

Staying power is not enough on its own to win the crown. When its turn comes, Immortal Romance gets the same treatment as every other game: the five points from [The Rules of the Contest]({% post_url 2017-09-02-The-Ace-of-Games %}), including how often its features really show up.
