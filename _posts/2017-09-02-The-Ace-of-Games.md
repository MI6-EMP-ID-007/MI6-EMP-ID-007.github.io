Before the first game steps into the ring, here is how the contest works.

**What we look at**

Every game is judged on the same five points, so the comparison is fair:

- **Return to player (RTP).** The share of all the money wagered on a game that it pays back over the long run. A higher RTP means a lower house edge. We explain it properly in [RTP and Volatility Explained]({% post_url 2017-09-03-The-Ace-of-Games %}).
- **Volatility.** How the wins are spread out: small and frequent, or rare and big. This decides how a game feels to play and how long your budget lasts.
- **Bonus features.** Free spins, multipliers, wilds and everything else that makes a game more than a spinning wheel. We care about how often features show up and how much they really add.
- **Playability.** Is the game easy to understand? Does it run smoothly on a phone? Are the bets flexible enough for small budgets?
- **The provider.** Games from established software providers are regularly tested by independent auditors. We prefer names with a track record.

**How the contest runs**

We review one game at a time and finish each review with a verdict on those five points. Once every contender has had its turn we will compare them side by side and name a champion. You can see who is in the running in [Meet the Contenders]({% post_url 2017-09-04-The-Ace-of-Games %}).

**One honest note**

No review, ours included, can tell you which spin will win. Slots are random, and over the long run the maths favours the casino. What we can do is help you choose games with fair odds and features you enjoy, and remind you to set a budget before you play and stick to it.
