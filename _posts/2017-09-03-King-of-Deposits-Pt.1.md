As part of our ongoing mission to help players around the globe become more successful in their online gambling endeavours we took it upon ourselves to test some of the most popular deposit methods that can be found online. This multipart test series will include an introductory post in which we present a short description of the methods that we are going to thoroughly test over the next few weeks.

<!--more-->

We will then test one Deposit method a week and submit our score. Ending it all with a side by side comparison of the pros and cons of the different deposit methods as well as declaring our overall winner.

This approach has several benefits to a singular extremely long but still less thorough review. One of them being that we might feel rushed into cutting corners when it comes to assessing the deposit method, which could lead to skewed results. Another would be that it can be unpleasant for our readers to have to read a single endless post about deposit methods and their various features. 

This is why we think a weekly test report of one deposit method is ideal. Best of all because we know that not everyone always has time to read even a short post, we will put the result right at the Top of the Post, so for all the readers who like their results immediately you need look no further than the very top of the post. 

But enough with all the rambling let's get to the meat of the matter, we have chosen to test the following 12 deposit methods:

1. **Visa:** the most widely accepted payment card.
2. **Mastercard:** the other card giant, accepted almost everywhere Visa is.
3. **PayPal:** a popular e-wallet that keeps your card details away from the casino. Not every casino accepts it.
4. **Skrill:** an e-wallet built for online payments and a common casino option.
5. **Neteller:** another widely used e-wallet, a close rival to Skrill.
6. **Paysafecard:** a prepaid voucher you buy with cash, so you can pay without sharing bank details. It is for deposits only.
7. **ecoPayz:** an e-wallet with a long history at online casinos.
8. **Bank transfer:** the traditional route, usually slower but often with high limits.
9. **Instant online banking:** services that pay straight from your bank account, such as Sofort or Trustly.
10. **Apple Pay:** pay with a card stored on your Apple device.
11. **Bitcoin:** a cryptocurrency that only a small number of casinos accept so far, and whose value can swing a lot.
12. **Pay by phone bill:** the charge goes on your phone bill, which is convenient but usually comes with low limits.

**What we will score**

Each method gets tested on the same six points:

- **Speed:** how fast the money arrives in your casino account.
- **Fees:** whether you or the casino pay anything.
- **Limits:** the minimum and maximum you can deposit.
- **Withdrawals:** whether you can also use the method to cash out.
- **Bonus eligibility:** whether deposits made this way qualify for welcome offers. Some casinos exclude certain methods, so it is worth checking.
- **Security and convenience:** how safe it feels and how easy it is to use.

Each weekly report puts the score right at the top of the post, as promised.
